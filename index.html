From code to product — and back again

我以前端工程師起步，建構了多語系網站和 LINE 小程式。這段親手實作的背景，帶著我走進現場工程、數據管道，最終進入產品管理——也讓我清楚知道，產品在每一層架構上是怎麼壞掉的。

現在我在 VMFive 主導 AdTech 產品，負責每日處理逾百萬次廣告曝光的 SSP/DSP 平台。我與工程、數據及商務團隊緊密合作，推動切實影響數字的功能——上線時間縮短、報表效率提升、跨團隊協作改善。

工作之外，我以創辦人兼 CEO 的身分打造 GoGo——一款 AI 旅遊規劃 App。從零到一帶領十人團隊的歷程，是我至今最有價值的產品課。

I started as a front-end developer, building multilingual websites and LINE mini-programs. That hands-on background led me into field engineering, then data pipelines, and eventually into product management — which means I understand exactly how products break at every layer of the stack.

Today I own the AdTech roadmap at VMFive, where I'm responsible for an SSP/DSP platform handling over a million daily ad impressions. I work closely with engineering, data, and commercial teams to ship features that move real numbers — launch-time reductions, reporting efficiency gains, cross-team coordination improvements.

Outside of work, I'm building GoGo — an AI-powered trip planning app — as Founder and CEO. Leading a 10-person team from zero to product has been the most valuable product education I've had.
